# Exploratory results — not primary confirmatory results

These files belong to the earlier exploratory run. They are retained to support the paper's secondary threshold-sensitivity discussion and to make their recorded outputs inspectable. They were not preregistered confirmatory results and must not be pooled with `confirmatory/results/authorization/`.

`decisions.csv`, summary/analysis CSVs, and `latency_summary.csv` are historical exploratory outputs. `threshold_sensitivity.csv` and `.png` show the secondary threshold grid described in `docs/robustness/threshold_sensitivity.md`. Their bytes were moved without recomputation. The optional Gemini integration was not executed and is excluded from this public artifact.
